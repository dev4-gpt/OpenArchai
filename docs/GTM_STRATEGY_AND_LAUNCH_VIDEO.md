## 1. Executive Summary: What AtelierOS Does

Architects and interior designers spend up to **60% of their billable hours** wrestling with fragmented, disconnected software:
* Drafting in **AutoCAD** or **Revit**
* 3D massing in **SketchUp** or **Rhino**
* Photorealistic rendering in **3ds Max / V-Ray** (taking 2–4 days per client revision)
* Budgeting and Schedule of Rates in **Microsoft Excel** (counted manually)
* Statutory checks flipping through 400-page **NBC 2016** PDF manuals
* Client reviews scattered across **WhatsApp threads** and unorganized PDFs

**AtelierOS replaces this 5-software stack with a unified, browser-native spatial operating system:**
1. **Intake & 2D Drafting**: Draw orthogonal floor plans with live dimensions or drag-and-drop AutoCAD `.dxf` files.
2. **Real-Time 3D & Materiality**: View 3D massing instantly. Swap real-world materials (Italian Statuario Marble ↔ Kota Stone ↔ Herringbone Oak) and simulate circadian sunlight (5000K morning ➔ 2700K evening recessed LEDs).
3. **Automated FF&E Procurement**: Furniture placement automatically populates an itemized spec sheet with real dimensions, vendor names (Jaquar, Asian Paints, Herman Miller), and localized market pricing in INR (₹) and USD ($).
4. **AI Architectural Studio Team**: Four specialized agents (Lead Architect, Building Code & Vastu Consultant, Interior Architect, and Quantity Surveyor) audit circulation, NBC 2016 clauses, and value engineering in parallel.
5. **Client Presentation Mode & Handover**: One-click switch into a full-screen branded client walkthrough with interactive 3D, cinematic video reel playback, and a 5-sheet high-res printable PDF dossier.

## 2. Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

### A. Target Personas
1. **Primary**: Boutique Interior Design Studios & Architecture Practices (3 to 25 designers).
2. **Secondary**: Turnkey Design-Build Residential Firms (need real-time material costing).
3. **Tertiary**: Independent Freelancers & Architectural Visualizers.

### B. Value Proposition
> *"Stop losing 3 days to every client revision. AtelierOS gives your studio real-time 3D, live code audits, and instant BOQs in one client-facing canvas."*

### C. Distribution & Acquisition Flywheel
1. **Frictionless Public Sandbox (Zero Login)**:
   - Anyone visiting [https://atelieros-cloud.vercel.app](https://atelieros-cloud.vercel.app) instantly lands in an interactive 1,200 sq ft Sample Studio Apartment. No registration wall.
2. **The "60-Second Studio Audit" Campaign**:
   - *"Drag your existing CAD floor plan here. In 60 seconds, get an interactive 3D model, Vastu orientation audit, NBC 2016 egress check, and an itemized finishing BOQ for free."*
3. **Viral Short-Form Content (LinkedIn & Instagram Reels)**:

4. **Institutional Education Partnerships**:
   - Free studio licenses for premier design schools (SPA Delhi, CEPT Ahmedabad, AA London, Pratt Institute) to establish organic, bottom-up student and alumni adoption.

### D. Pricing Model
